Methods

Arps hyperbolic decline q = qi / (1 + b·Di·t)^(1/b), switching to exponential decline at a terminal rate Dmin. Rate is per day, Di is nominal annual decline. Economics are undiscounted-royalty, pre-tax net revenue: volume × (price − opex) minus fixed costs, discounted monthly. Units are whatever you enter (bbl/d with $/bbl, or mcf/d with $/mcf).
