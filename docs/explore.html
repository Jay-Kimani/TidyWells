How to read this. Rates are monthly volume divided by calendar days, so short months do not look low. Gas has a season (see Gas has a season), so month-on-month gas moves are partly the calendar. Alberta oil includes bitumen. The seasonally adjusted view divides each month by the typical seasonal pattern for that calendar month, estimated from the ratio to a centred 12-month average (the method behind Gas has a season); it is an estimate from under five years of data. These are sums of public well-level rows, not official provincial statistics, and operators can revise earlier months. Sources: Petrinex (Alberta, Saskatchewan) and the BC Energy Regulator.
