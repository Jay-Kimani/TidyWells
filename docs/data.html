Open data

Free monthly production totals for Alberta, Saskatchewan and British Columbia, aggregated from public regulator data. No sign-up. Use it in a spreadsheet, a notebook or a story, and cite Tidywells and the regulators. Archived with a permanent DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23969867.

Download

tidywells-monthly-production.csv

ColumnMeaning
provinceAB, SK or BC
productOIL or GAS
monthProduction month, YYYY-MM
volumeTotal reported volume for the month
unitm3 for oil (cubic metres); 10^3 m3 for gas (thousand cubic metres)
wells_producingWells reporting a volume above zero that month
days_in_monthCalendar days, for per-day rates

Sources and caveats

  • Alberta and Saskatchewan: Petrinex public data. British Columbia: BC Energy Regulator public data.
  • Oil here is the regulator's oil product, so Alberta oil includes bitumen. Provinces report in slightly different ways, so compare trends more than exact levels.
  • Regulators revise past months, so a later copy can differ slightly from an earlier one. The file is rebuilt every month.
  • Totals only, no individual wells. Informational, not legal, engineering or investment advice, and provided as is.

How we check this

Every time the site is rebuilt, a script recomputes the figures from this file and compares them with what the pages say. Last run: 800 figures across 33 monthly snapshot pages and the home page, 0 mismatches. The file has 342 rows (6 series, 2022-01 to 2026-09), with no missing months, duplicates or negative volumes.

File fingerprint (SHA-256): 94193a121eff937329b691e179f73bd2d545478244e76e3d95796f754ce60bfb · manifest.json · the checking script. This confirms the pages match the data file. It does not prove the regulator files themselves are right, which is why the caveats above matter.
