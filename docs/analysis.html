Latest and recent
November 2026How fast do new wells decline in their first year?

A typical new Alberta oil well is producing about 40% of its early peak rate a year in; Saskatchewan oil 38%, Alberta gas 50%. The shape barely changed from 2022 to 2025.

November 2026What a naive load gets wrong: traps in Canadian well files

Default CSV loading drops leading zeros from codes, counts 13,735 extra Alberta well rows, and splits licence statuses into 16 spellings instead of 8.

November 2026Gas-oil ratios: an "oil well" is not the same everywhere

Alberta crude-oil wells have a median gas-oil ratio of about 1,896 scf/bbl, Saskatchewan about 693, and Alberta bitumen wells about 352.

November 2026How concentrated is production? Licensee shares in Alberta and Saskatchewan

The five largest licensees hold 52% of Alberta's producing wells and 70% of Saskatchewan's; Saskatchewan's largest alone holds 27% of oil volume.

November 2026Are new wells getting better? First-six-month rates by year

Alberta oil wells that first produced in 2025 started 38% stronger than 2022 starters at the median; Alberta gas is noisier and Saskatchewan oil edged up. Corrected 4 November 2026.

November 2026Shut-ins and restarts: how many wells stop, and do they come back?

Of the wells producing in January 2022, 84% in Alberta, 82% in Saskatchewan and 73% in BC were still producing in September 2026; about one well in ten had a gap of three months or more.

November 2026Most licensed wells aren't producing

Alberta's well file lists about 668,000 wells and about 143,000 reported production in the last year. Saskatchewan: 158,000 and 42,000.

November 2026Gas has a season. Oil mostly doesn't.

Alberta and BC gas run 6 to 10% above trend in winter and below it through summer and early autumn. Oil barely moves with the calendar.

November 2026Why the decline curve runs low: wells that bounce back

About 35 to 50% of wells recover after the training window. A low month just before the forecast multiplies the typical miss by two to five, but does not explain all of it.

November 2026How well does a simple decline curve forecast Canadian wells?

A backtest of a bounded Arps decline curve on about 1,900 Alberta, Saskatchewan and BC wells: how accurate a 12-month forecast is, how much history you need, and why the curve runs low.
