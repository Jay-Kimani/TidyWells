Tidywells Pro

Monthly subscriptionCancel any time

The free site stays free: the decline-curve tool, the open-source package and the biweekly analysis posts. Pro is for people who want the cleaned numbers behind them each month without doing the loading themselves.

What our subscription includes

  • Monthly data pack. Tidy aggregate CSVs for Alberta, Saskatchewan and BC: monthly oil and gas volumes with wells reporting and per-well averages, plus a well-status table and a decline-curve backtest summary, built with the methods on this site.
  • Companion tables. The numbers behind the public analysis posts as CSVs: shut-ins and survival, new-well vintages, licensee concentration, gas-oil ratio and the oil-versus-bitumen backtest.
  • Questions answered. Reply to any email with a data question about the files or the loaders.

To be clear about what this is: the monthly volumes in the data pack come from the same public tables as the free monthly snapshots. The pack adds structure and convenience (tidy CSVs, per-well and growth columns, consistent units), not new data.
