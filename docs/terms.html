Privacy

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address is stored by Buttondown to send you posts. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Payments: card and billing details are handled by Stripe. Tidywells does not see or store your card number.
  • Website: this site does not use advertising trackers. Files you load into the decline-curve tool stay in your browser.
  • Comments: if you comment, the name and comment you enter are stored by Tidywells (on Cloudflare) and shown publicly once approved. We store a one-way hash of your IP address with your comment to limit spam; Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot. Do not post personal information. To have a comment removed, email us.
  • Analytics: if enabled, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or track people across sites.
  • Sponsors: any sponsor strip is labelled and links out to the sponsor's own site, which has its own privacy practices.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We use it only to deliver what you asked for and to answer your messages. To access or delete your information, email us.
