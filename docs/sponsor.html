Sponsor Tidywells

Tidywells publishes plain-language analysis of public Canadian oil and gas data, free tools and an open-source package. A sponsor helps keep the free parts free.

What a sponsorship is

  • One clearly labelled "Sponsor" strip at the top of every page for the sponsorship period, with your name, a one-line message and a link.
  • One sponsor at a time, so yours is the only one.
  • Suitable for companies that serve this audience, such as data, software, consulting and services firms in the energy sector.

What it is not

  • Sponsors do not review or influence the analysis, and sponsorship does not change what we publish.
  • No pop-ups, no tracking pixels and no email promotion: the newsletter stays free of sales messages.
  • We do not make guarantees about visitor numbers or results. We can share our site analytics with you before you decide.
