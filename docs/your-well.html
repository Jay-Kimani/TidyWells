Your Well poster

Runs in your browser; nothing is uploadedFor wells you own or may use

Turn one well's monthly production into a poster: a clean line, the peak, the latest month and the change since the start. Paste two columns (month and volume), load a file, or load the output of the File cleaner and choose a well.

Use only data you own or have the right to print. Tidywells does not sell prints of other people's wells made from public files: the regulators' terms need confirming in writing first. The poster uses the fonts on your device, so it can look slightly different from the shop posters.
