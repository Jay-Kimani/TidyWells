Forecast scorecard

Most sites show forecasts and never check them. We write simple forecasts of provincial oil and gas production before the data exists, then grade them against what the regulators report. The methods are deliberately plain, so you can see how hard the problem is.

What the backtest shows

Across 12 series and horizons the lowest error went to: last month repeated 6, seasonal trend 4, same month last year 2. Averaged over everything, the best method was last month repeated at 4.7% mean error (seasonal trend 5.1%, same month last year 6.1%). If a fancier method cannot beat these, it has not earned your trust.

The rules

  • Forecasts are written to an append-only log. Each commit is dated by GitHub, so none can be quietly rewritten.
  • A script, not a person, fills in the actual number when the month appears in the open data.
  • Error is absolute percentage error on the daily rate. Data can be revised; see Data revisions.

The three methods

  • Seasonal trend. Same month last year, scaled by how the last 3 months compared with a year earlier.
  • Same month last year. The value from the same month a year earlier, unchanged.
  • Last month repeated. The most recent month, carried forward.
