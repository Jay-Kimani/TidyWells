Forecast scorecard

Most sites show forecasts and never check them. Here we write simple forecasts of provincial oil and gas production down before the data exists, then grade them against what the regulators later report. The methods are deliberately plain, so you can see how hard the problem really is.

The rules

  • Forecasts are made from the latest published month and written to an append-only log. Each commit is dated by GitHub, so we cannot quietly rewrite one.
  • A script, not a person, fills in the actual number when the month appears in the open data, and calculates the error.
  • Errors are absolute percentage errors on the daily rate. The data can be revised after first publication; see Data revisions.

The three methods

  • Seasonal trend. Same month last year, scaled by how the last 3 months compared with a year earlier.
  • Same month last year. The value from the same month a year earlier, unchanged.
  • Last month repeated. The most recent month, carried forward.

What this shows

Across 12 series and horizon combinations the lowest error went to: last month repeated 6, seasonal trend 4, same month last year 2. Averaged over everything, the best method was last month repeated at 4.7% mean error (seasonal trend 5.1%, same month last year 6.1%). If a fancier method cannot beat these, it has not earned your trust.
