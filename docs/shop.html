Good to know

Is this affiliated with a regulator or an operator?
No. The art uses public regulator data and original wording. No company names or logos appear on any product.

Are the numbers on the posters right?
They are computed from the open dataset and checked against it in our tests. Oil and gas are the Petrinex conventional volumetrics products, so totals can differ from other published provincial figures. The posters are not investment advice.

Who makes and ships the products?
A print-on-demand partner, confirmed when the store opens. Sizes, delivery times and prices will be on each listing.

Where does the money go?
It helps keep the open data, tools and analysis free. Tidywells is built by one person; see About.
