Good to know

How do sizes fit?
True to size. Every hoodie and tee page has a size guide with flat measurements.

Who makes and ships the products?
Our fulfilment partner. Production and delivery times follow the partner schedule and are shown at checkout.

Is this affiliated with a regulator or an operator?
No. The posters use public regulator data and original wording, and no company names or logos appear on any product. Posters are not investment advice.

Where does the money go?
It helps keep the open data, tools and analysis free. Tidywells is built by one person; see About.
